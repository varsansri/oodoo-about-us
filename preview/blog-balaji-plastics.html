Odoo ERP Implementation and Odoo 16 to Odoo 19 Migration at Sri Balaji Plastics, Coimbatore

Sri Balaji Plastics, Coimbatore, is a prominent manufacturer and supplier of a wide range of superior-quality Leo Taps and fittings. With growing business operations and increasing transaction volumes, the company needed a robust ERP platform to streamline its sales, procurement, inventory, manufacturing, and invoicing processes.

Company Sri Balaji Plastics
Industry Plastic Taps & Fittings
Headquarters Coimbatore, India
Odoo Version Odoo 19

Challenges

As the business expanded, Sri Balaji Plastics faced challenges in managing its operations efficiently:

  • Separate tools and processes for Sales, Purchase, Inventory, and Manufacturing
  • Difficulty in tracking and managing Manufacturing Orders and production status
  • Limited visibility into real-time inventory and product availability
  • Challenges in maintaining accurate customer and transaction history
  • Limited coordination between Sales, Purchase, Inventory, and Production teams
  • Manual effort involved in tracking orders, production, deliveries, and invoicing
  • Difficulty obtaining real-time business information for management decision-making
  • The existing Odoo 16 environment required an upgrade to leverage the newer features, improved performance, security, and capabilities available in the latest Odoo version

The company required an integrated ERP solution that could provide end-to-end visibility across its business operations while also ensuring a smooth transition from Odoo 16 to Odoo 19.

Solution Delivered

Oodu Implementers implemented and upgraded the Odoo ERP platform for Sri Balaji Plastics, bringing key business functions onto a single integrated system.

Key Solutions Delivered

    Odoo 16 to Odoo 19 Version Migration

  • Migrated the existing Odoo environment from Version 16 to Version 19
  • Ensured continuity of business operations during the migration
  • Migrated relevant business data and configurations
  • Validated migrated data and business processes after the upgrade
  • Enabled the client to leverage the latest Odoo capabilities

    Sales & Invoicing

  • Centralized sales order management
  • Improved quotation-to-invoice workflow
  • Better visibility of customer transactions and sales history

    Purchase & Vendor Management

  • Streamlined purchase processes
  • Improved vendor and purchase order management
  • Better visibility into procurement requirements

    Inventory Management

  • Centralized inventory information
  • Improved product availability tracking
  • Better visibility of stock movements and inventory status

    Manufacturing

  • Structured manufacturing order management
  • Improved production tracking
  • Better visibility into assembly and production status
  • Connected manufacturing activities with inventory requirements

    Integrated Business Operations

  • Connected Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Manufacturing, and Accounting
  • Created a single source of truth for operational and management information

Key Benefits Achieved

  • Successfully upgraded from Odoo 16 to Odoo 19
  • Improved system performance and usability
  • Faster and more organized invoicing cycles
  • Better control over manufacturing operations
  • Improved inventory visibility and stock control
  • More organized customer and transaction history
  • Better control over purchases and vendor management
  • Improved coordination between departments
  • Single source of truth for management

Business Impact

The upgraded and integrated Odoo platform helped Sri Balaji Plastics achieve greater operational visibility and process efficiency. The transformation resulted in:

  • Improved on-time product delivery through better production and inventory visibility
  • Reduced revenue leakage through structured sales and invoicing processes
  • Faster customer response times with centralized customer and order information
  • Better profitability visibility across sales and business operations
  • Streamlined internal collaboration between Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Manufacturing, and Accounts
  • Improved management decision-making through centralized and real-time business information
  • A future-ready ERP environment with the successful migration to Odoo 19

Client Testimonial

“Odoo brought our Sales, Manufacturing, Purchase, and Inventory onto one platform. The migration to Odoo 19 has further improved our visibility and control over our operations, helping us manage production and serve our customers better.”
— Team Sri Balaji Plastics

Conclusion

The Odoo implementation and Odoo 16 to Odoo 19 migration by Oodu Implementers enabled Sri Balaji Plastics to move from fragmented processes to a connected and future-ready ERP environment.

By integrating Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Manufacturing, and Invoicing into a single platform, the company gained better operational visibility, improved accountability, streamlined collaboration, and enhanced customer service.

The successful migration to Odoo 19 also positioned Sri Balaji Plastics with a modern ERP platform capable of supporting its continued business growth and digital transformation.
